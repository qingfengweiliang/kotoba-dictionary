Data extraction: Kaikki.org / Wiktextract, Tatu Ylonen and contributors.
https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/ (Chinese/English/Japanese definitions in Chinese)
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Japanese/ (Japanese definitions in English)
The sources and retrieval hashes are recorded in data/expansion-meta.json.
Original page titles are retained per entry as zhSources / enSources (or sources).
App source controls open the original page, whose history lists individual contributors.
Page URLs: https://zh.wiktionary.org/wiki/{encoded title}
           https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/{encoded title}
Append ?action=history to view the corresponding author history.
Changes: selected definitions, readings and written variants; Chinese definitions and
Chinese headwords normalized to Simplified Chinese using OpenCC; Japanese headwords
left unchanged. Entries merged by language and lexical identity; additional entries and
reading/spelling/Pinyin/romaji aliases indexed in SQLite. No machine translation used.
